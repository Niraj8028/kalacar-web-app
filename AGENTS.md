# Kalacar UI implementation guidance

Before building or materially changing a storefront or dashboard screen, review the matching examples in ../kalacar_obsedian_voulte/kalacar_obsedian_voulte/assets/ui-inspiration/README.md. Compare patterns by purpose (navigation, product discovery, product detail, responsive layout) and choose the combination that best fits Kalacar's small devotional-idol shop. Do not copy a source website's logo, text, imagery, or exact layout. Use the references to inform an original, simpler design, and use only owner-provided product facts and authorized imagery.

Follow the ordered work and scope in ../kalacar_obsedian_voulte/kalacar_obsedian_voulte/16-progress-checklist.md. Testing is deferred to its QA stage as the owner requested; do not claim a screen is verified before then.
